# Floating Editorial Re-composition — Phase 4 Final Certification Baseline

## Status

**Canonical visual-certification and polish baseline after Phase 4.**

Phase 4 is the closeout layer for the Floating Editorial programme established in Phases 1–3.

It does not introduce a new design system or reopen page identities. It records the evidence-based polish applied after fresh rendered certification and freezes the resulting responsive state.

---

## 1. Governing rule

Phase 4 follows one principle:

> **Polish only where rendered evidence shows a specific imbalance or regression. Otherwise leave the established identity unchanged.**

Every change must:
- remain surface-specific;
- preserve the Phase 3 composition identity;
- keep mobile, laptop and workstation as distinct responsive compositions;
- add or preserve regression coverage;
- avoid speculative cleanup or broad stylistic rewrites.

## 6. Freeze rules after Phase 4

The following are now considered stable design decisions:
- page-specific identities from Phase 3;
- Phase 4 mobile masthead/Index polish;
- INIBIC glass-plaque material hierarchy;
- Publications empty-state rhythm;
- Team hero restraint;
- existing Research and Innovation compositions.

Future work should not casually:
- reopen these identities;
- introduce generic cards;
- broaden floating surfaces;
- rescale workstation typography;
- collapse distinct responsive compositions into one scaled layout;
- replace regression tests with weaker assertions.

Any later redesign should be treated as a new milestone with explicit rationale, evidence and regression coverage.

The next milestone should focus on product/content/system goals rather than continuing Phase 4 visual tweaking by default.
