# Phase 7.5 — Team Portrait & Roster Polish

## Purpose

Refine the accepted Phase 7.3 multidisciplinary hierarchy and Phase 7.4 illustrated hero without changing the Team information architecture.

## Changes

- Keeps the rabbit multidisciplinary hero unchanged.
- Adds explicit focal-position handling for approved public portraits.
- Uses smaller, face-safe leadership framing and consistent 4:5 coordinator portraits.
- Upgrades initials fallbacks so they read as intentional editorial placeholders.
- Promotes the complete team to a two-column first-class roster with larger portrait slots and clearer professional metadata.
- Keeps roster surfaces non-hoverable; only real links retain interaction feedback.
- Restores a literal `Complete team / Equipo completo` heading.
- Aligns Team footer density with the accepted Landing footer and closes remaining shared footer selector gaps.

## Data

No API contract changes. `/api/team/website` and research-line relationship enrichment remain authoritative. No synthetic portraits are introduced.

## Visual rule

Leadership and coordination are responsibilities within one team. Portrait size communicates role context only; it must not imply that the complete roster is secondary.
