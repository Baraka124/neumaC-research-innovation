# Phase 7.7 — Team Editorial Profile Sheet

## Purpose
Give the remaining multidisciplinary team members useful depth without turning the Team page back into a directory or duplicating the deeper research-line treatment already available for programme leadership and line coordinators.

## Changes
- The multidisciplinary roster cards are now deliberate profile triggers.
- Research-line links are removed from the compact roster surface and moved into the profile sheet, reducing any suggestion of fixed line ownership.
- A right-side editorial profile sheet opens on demand and preserves the underlying Team page context.
- The sheet renders only governed public data: portrait/initials, role, specialty, affiliation, public biography when present, current research relationships and ORCID/Scholar/ResearchGate links when present.
- Missing biography or scholarly links simply disappear; no placeholder biography is invented.
- Escape, backdrop click, close button, keyboard focus trapping and focus restoration are supported.
- On small screens the sheet becomes a full-width reading surface.

## Scope
Pedro and the named research-line coordinators do not use this sheet. Their deeper scientific context remains attached to their research-line pages.
