# Phase 5.8 — Accepted Research Editorial System

This phase implements the approved Research-page direction after the visual prototyping rounds.

## Locked top-of-page structure

1. Short light editorial hero with a replaceable respiratory-research image.
2. Featured Principal Investigator / scientific leadership block for Pedro Jorge Marcos Rodríguez.
3. Compact research-line rows with replaceable editorial media, line-scoped descriptions, one coordination column and calm detail-page links.
4. Existing editorial study portfolio, research context, collaboration disclosure and canonical footer continue below.

## Principal Investigator block

Pedro is presented once as programme-wide Principal Investigator and scientific leadership. This does not replace his line-specific role in Research Line 6.

The public feature uses a concise editorial biography and four evidence signals supplied for the profile:

- Global Clinical Scholar Research Training Program, Harvard Medical School.
- 25+ research projects.
- 100+ scientific publications.
- 9 research awards or grants.

The portrait uses the real institutional-photo reference supplied during the redesign discussion.

## Research-line row contract

Each overview row contains only line-scoped information:

- replaceable editorial image;
- up to four focus terms;
- official research-line title;
- concise backend description;
- coordinator name;
- explicit link to the dedicated line page.

Active-study counts and project inventories are intentionally excluded from these rows. Long live descriptions receive a labelled read-more disclosure rather than silent truncation.

## Media ownership

All Research-page editorial media is owned by `assets/research/`.

Synthetic images are placeholders and can be replaced later without changing public markup or data contracts. `assets/research/README.md` records the replacement contract.

## Interaction tone

The research-line arrow is deliberately small and low-contrast. It supports the action rather than acting as visual decoration.
