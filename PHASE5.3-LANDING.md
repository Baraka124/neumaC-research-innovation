# Phase 5.3 — Editorial Landing Page

Phase 5.3 moves the landing page from a polished directory toward an editorial institutional front door.

## Locked decisions

- Public institutional identity uses **Área Sanitaria da Coruña e Cee**, not the narrower hospital acronym.
- The homepage no longer repeats the six research-line taxonomy. Research lines remain available in the global Research mega-menu and the Research portfolio.
- The former research atlas is replaced by **“The clinical question is the starting point / La pregunta clínica es el punto de partida.”**
- The new identity section explains the multidisciplinary group and the Observe → Investigate → Design → Evaluate → Transfer logic.
- “Now at neumACt” remains backend-driven and is framed as current governed public activity.
- Agenda remains ready for future internal research system-controlled public events without inventing dates or factual events.
- Innovation is reframed around why innovation matters in a respiratory clinical environment, with one governed project spotlight.
- Institutional context becomes a collaboration ecosystem: home environment + external collaborator types + patient/public-service purpose.
- Homepage contact uses native progressive disclosure; the form is closed until the visitor chooses to start a conversation.
- Footer is reduced to identity, navigation, connection and legal information. Decorative lighthouse and unearned compliance strips are removed from the shared visual system.
