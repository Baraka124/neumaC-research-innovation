# PHASE 7.6.3 — TEAM HERO COMPOSITION POLISH

## Goal
Finalise the approved A Coruña rabbit-team hero as part of the page composition rather than as a standalone banner.

## Changes
- Tuned desktop/tablet/mobile hero framing.
- Widened the tablet crop so more of the coastal Coruña context survives without displacing the wooden lung as the focal point.
- Reduced the navy top wash and frosted bottom wash so the city and sea remain visible but quiet.
- Added a controlled overlap between the hero and the `Our team / Nuestro equipo` introduction, echoing the integrated image/content treatment used in high-end clinical editorial sites.
- Kept Coruña implicit in the image; no tourist-style landmark label or extra location copy was added.
- Preserved the Phase 7.6.1 copy-discipline and Team hierarchy unchanged.

## Rule
The image supplies atmosphere and local context. The page copy supplies facts. Neither should repeat the other.
